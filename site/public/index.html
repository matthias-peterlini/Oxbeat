Your computer is already a piano.

In Oxbeat the letters on your keyboard play notes, so you can start a song before you own any gear. Try it right here: press A to K, or tap the keys. Z and X change octave.

A whole studio, none of the homework.

The first thing you see is a track that already makes sound. Everything else is one click away.

  1. Play

    Plug in a MIDI keyboard and it just works. No keyboard? Use the computer keys or the on-screen keys and drum pads.

  2. Record

    Record what you play as notes, or your voice and guitar through a microphone, with a level meter and monitoring.

  3. Sounds

    Built-in synths and drum kits. Load SoundFont (SF2), SFZ and plain audio files from your own disk.

  4. Edit

    Move, stretch, split and duplicate clips. Fix notes in the piano roll, program beats on a drum grid, quantize in one click.

  5. Effects

    Reverb, delay, EQ, compressor, distortion, chorus, gate, de-esser and pitch correction, plus one-click vocal presets.

  6. Mix

    A fader, pan and meter for every track, and a limiter on the master so a loud song never distorts.

  7. Keep time

    Metronome, a one-bar count-in before you record, and a loop range for practising a section.

  8. Share

    Export the song as MP3 for friends or WAV in full quality, with reverb and echo left to ring out.

  9. Undo

    Every change can be undone and redone, so trying an idea never costs you anything.

  10. Own it

    A project is a plain folder holding a readable project file and your recordings. Nothing lives in a cloud.
