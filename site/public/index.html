Everything you need

A whole studio, without the learning curve.

Play

Plug in a MIDI keyboard and it just works, or play from the computer keyboard, on-screen keys and drum pads.

Sounds

Built-in synths and drum kits, plus a browser that loads SoundFont (SF2), SFZ and plain audio files.

Sound packs

Download free pianos, drums, saxophones, flutes and a full General MIDI set from inside the app.

Record

Record what you play as notes, or your voice and guitar through a microphone, with a level meter and monitoring.

Edit

Move, stretch, split and duplicate clips. Fix notes in a piano roll, program beats on a drum grid, quantize in one click.

Effects

Reverb, delay, EQ, compressor, distortion, chorus, gate, de-esser and pitch correction, with one-click vocal presets.

Mix

Faders, pan and meters for every track, and a master limiter so a loud song never distorts.

Stay in time

A metronome, a count-in before you record, and a loop range for practising one section over and over.

Share

Export the whole song as MP3 or WAV, with the reverb and echo tails left to ring out.

Relax

Undo and redo everything. Try ideas freely: nothing you do is permanent.

Take the tour

Clear screens, big controls.

Lay out your song: every instrument and voice gets its own lane of clips.

Free, for real

No account. No subscription. No catch.

Oxbeat is free software under the GNU GPL. Nobody can take it away from you, lock your songs behind a paywall or show you ads. Your project is a plain folder on your computer, with your recordings inside.

  • Works offline, no sign-in
  • The same app on Windows, macOS and Linux
  • Written in Rust, with a real-time audio engine built for low latency
  • Open code anyone can read, fix and improve
